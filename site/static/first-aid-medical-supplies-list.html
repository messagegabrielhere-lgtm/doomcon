Questions about first aid and medical supplies

What should a home first aid kit include?

Bandages and gauze in several sizes, tape, gloves, scissors, tweezers, antiseptic wipes, a thermometer, cold packs and any medicines your household takes, plus a first aid guide.

Should I learn first aid?

Yes. Supplies help only if you know how to use them. The Red Cross runs first aid and CPR classes, and Stop the Bleed teaches bleeding control.

How often should I check my first aid kit?

Every six months: replace anything used, expired or damaged, and refresh medicines by their dates.
