Questions about lighting

Are candles safe in a power outage?

Ready.gov and the American Red Cross recommend flashlights and battery lanterns instead of candles, which are a fire risk.

How many flashlights do I need?

At least one per person, kept by each bed, plus a lantern for a shared room and spare batteries for all of them.

What light lasts longest without batteries?

Solar and hand-crank lights recharge without buying batteries; a rechargeable light can also be topped up from a power bank or solar panel.
