How we know it's real

Deepfakes of Elon are good enough that you can't judge one by looking at it. So this index doesn't look. It only lists videos uploaded by the channel that recorded them: his companies, hosts who interviewed him, and newsrooms with their own cameras.

A video is listed when its title or description names him, or when it's an official company event he presents (shareholder meetings, product unveils, Starship updates). The index refreshes every hour and keeps everything it has seen.

Signs of a fake

  • A "live" stream promising to double crypto you send, or a QR code to claim a giveaway. Elon has never run one.
  • A channel renamed to "Tesla" or "SpaceX" with a different handle than the real one.
  • A clip only posted by a fan, "news" or compilation account, with no original source linked.
  • Lip movements that drift from the words, or a voice that's flat on emotional lines.
