Questions about knives and multitools

Fixed blade or folding knife?

A fixed blade is stronger for camp work; a folding knife is easier to carry every day. Many kits carry a multitool plus one fixed blade.

Why keep a sharpener?

A dull blade needs more force and slips more often. A small sharpener keeps every knife in the kit useful.

Are there knife laws to know?

Yes. Blade length and carry rules differ by state and city. Check local law before carrying a knife.
