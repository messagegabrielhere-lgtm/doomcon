Questions about pets

What does Ready.gov say a pet kit needs?

Food and water for several days, medicines, a first aid kit, collar with ID and a leash, a crate or carrier, sanitation supplies, a picture of you with your pet and familiar items.

Will an evacuation shelter take my pet?

Many shelters accept only service animals. Find pet-friendly shelters, hotels or friends along your route ahead of time.

How much water does a pet need?

It varies by size; plan extra beyond the human gallon per day. The free water calculator includes pets.
