Questions about power stations, solar and generators

What size power station do I need?

Add up the watts each device draws times the hours you run it each day; that total in watt-hours is your daily need. The free off-grid power budget spreadsheet does the maths and shows how much solar covers it.

Where is it safe to run a generator?

Outside only. The CDC says never run one inside a home, garage, basement or crawlspace, and keep it at least 20 feet from windows, doors and vents.

Can I plug a generator into my house wiring?

Only through a transfer switch or interlock installed by a licensed electrician. Backfeeding through an outlet can electrocute utility workers and damage the house.
