Questions about clothing and cold weather

What clothes should go in a go-bag?

Ready.gov lists a complete change of clothing per person, including long sleeves, long pants and sturdy shoes, chosen for your climate.

Why wool instead of cotton?

Cotton loses most of its warmth when wet, while wool keeps insulating when damp. Layers let you add and remove warmth.

What gear protects you during cleanup?

Sturdy boots, heavy work gloves, eye protection and a mask for dust or mould, per FEMA cleanup guidance.
