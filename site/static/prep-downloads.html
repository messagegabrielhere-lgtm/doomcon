Questions

Are these downloads free?

Yes. No sign-up and no email. Download, print and share them.

What opens the spreadsheets?

Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers or LibreOffice. The CSV opens in anything.

Where do the numbers come from?

Water follows Ready.gov: at least one gallon per person per day. Every other planning figure is marked as an assumption inside its file, in a yellow cell you can change.
