Questions about bags and kits

What is a bug-out bag?

A packed bag that holds what one person needs for about 72 hours away from home: water, food, light, radio, first aid, documents and clothing. The free 72-hour kit checklist lists it.

Should each person have their own bag?

Yes. Each person, including children, carries a kit sized for them, so supplies are not lost if the family is separated.

How often should I check a go-bag?

Every six months: replace food and water, check batteries and medicines, and update clothes for the season and for growing children.
