Questions about babies and kids

What baby supplies should I store?

Formula (or ready-to-feed formula, which needs no water), bottles, diapers, wipes, rash cream and any medicines, for at least two weeks at home.

How do I prepare kids for an emergency?

Ready.gov suggests teaching them family phone numbers and meeting places, practising the plan, and letting them help pack their own kit.

Why pack toys and books?

Familiar comfort items and quiet activities ease stress during long outages and shelter stays.
