Questions about navigation and signaling

Why carry a paper map?

Phones lose signal and battery. A paper map of your area and evacuation routes works in any outage.

What is the distress signal with a whistle?

Three blasts, repeated, is the widely recognised signal for help. A whistle carries much farther than a voice.

Do I need a GPS if I have a phone?

A handheld GPS works without cell service and has longer battery life; a phone with offline maps is a good backup.
