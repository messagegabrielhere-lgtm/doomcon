  • The gap log records every time a market's edge crosses your threshold while this page is open: when it opened, how big it got and how long it lasted. Gaps that close in a few seconds are usually stale quotes or the model lagging, and they're gone before a person could act.
    • 
      
  • This is the "compare live BTC with Polymarket odds" idea as a read-only monitor. It never places orders.
    • 
      
  • Model: BTC follows a random walk with the volatility above (by default, the last 7 days of hourly OKX returns). Real prices have fatter tails, so a big "edge" on a far-away strike usually means the model is wrong, not the market.
    • 
      
  • Polymarket settles on Binance BTC/USDT or Chainlink; this page uses the OKX price, which can differ by a few dollars. Spreads, fees and slippage come out of any edge.
    • 
      
  • The 5-minute, 15-minute and 4-hour Up/Down markets settle on an average price over the window, which the model doesn't price; they're listed but not scored.
    • 
      
  • Polymarket's main exchange isn't available to people in the US and some other countries. Check the rules where you live.
