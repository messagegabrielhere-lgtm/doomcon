Stress index

0–100 per country, recomputed every time a source lands. Three parts, each 0–100:

News — headlines from the last 24 hours that name the country, weighted by category (conflict and security 3, nuclear 2.5, unrest and disasters 2, health and migration 1.5, economy, energy, cyber and shipping 1, diplomacy 0.5), then min(100, 25·log₂(1+w)).

Hazard — M4.5+ earthquakes in the last 7 days inside or near the border (energy-weighted), GDACS orange/red alerts and open NASA EONET events.

Market — only where a US-listed country ETF exists: 5-day drawdown plus 20-day volatility against its own year.

Composite = 0.55·News + 0.30·Hazard + 0.15·Market, re-weighted when a part is missing. The arrow is the change since this browser first scored the country today. It counts coverage, not danger: a country nobody writes about in English scores low.
