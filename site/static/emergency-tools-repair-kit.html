Questions about tools and repair

When should I turn off the gas?

Ready.gov says only if you smell gas or suspect a leak. Once off, only the gas company should turn it back on. Learn where your gas, water and power shut-offs are now.

What tool shuts off utilities?

A combination gas and water shut-off wrench fits most meters. Keep it next to the valve.

How do I patch a damaged roof?

A heavy tarp, nailed or strapped over the damage, keeps rain out until a roofer arrives. Stay off a roof in wind or rain.
