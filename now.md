## What SIREN is and isn't

- Is: Every AI doom number is a judgement call. This one is arithmetic: how much is happening in AI, measured hourly from public data — not how bad it is. Five pillars, one 0-100 score, one level from 5 (quietest) to 1 (loudest), and every number reproducible from a published receipt.
- Isn't: SIREN measures how much is happening, not how bad it is. Levels run from 5 (quietest) to 1 (loudest) and describe observable activity tempo against this index’s own record. They are not a probability of harm.
- Cadence: the score is computed hourly; the newsroom and alerts refresh every few minutes.
