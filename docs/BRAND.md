# BRAND — the marks, the icons, the palette

The identity of the **AI Early Warning System**: one logo, one icon grid, one
set of principal emblems, one palette with a hue per job. Everything here is
generated by code in this repo and every number in it was measured on
2026-09-24 with the harness described in §6.

The rule this file is downstream of is `docs/VOICE.md` §1 — *AIpocalypse in the
voice, rigour in the number*. A mark is a voice surface. It may be vivid. It may
not make a claim the engine cannot support, and it may never be the only thing
carrying a reading.

Files:

```
site/brandmarks.mjs            logo, favicon, touch icons, manifest, OG image
site/templates/_icons.mjs      the icon sprite: 49 marks in 8 sets
site/templates/_avatars.mjs    the 8 principal emblems + the honest empty frame
site/styles.mjs                the paint (owned elsewhere; §4 is the contract)
```

## 1. The logo — a detector, not a clock

**Every other instrument in this category draws a clock.** The Doomsday Clock,
the IMD AI Safety Clock, skynetcountdown's countdown. A clock is a statement
about how much time is left, which is a statement about the future, which is the
one sentence `VOICE.md` §3.1 bars us from writing. Drawing one would be breaking
our own ban in the largest type on the site.

The honest picture of "early warning" is the one `site/brand.mjs` already argues
in prose: **a smoke detector**. It predicts nothing. It has no opinion about the
fire. It tells you sooner than you would otherwise have known, and it is judged
on whether it is wired to anything. That is exactly the claim `docs/NEWS.md`
opens with — we read arXiv, Hugging Face, the lab feeds and the filings
directly, so we are upstream of the accounts that relay them.

So the mark is a detector seen face-on.

| element | what it is | what it carries |
|---|---|---|
| outer ring | the housing | nothing; it is the frame |
| five slots | the grille, swept across the lower arc | one per DOOMCON level, 5 on the left to 1 on the right |
| central LED | the lamp | the current level, in its heat colour |

### 1.1 The favicon carries the reading

The favicon takes the level and **lights every slot from the calm end up to the
current one** — two lit at DOOMCON 4, five lit at DOOMCON 1. The tab icon *is*
the instrument.

Nobody in this category does this. pizzint ships a static flag; DoomBench, the
IMD clock and skynetcountdown ship a static logo. `docs/COMPETITIVE.md` §1.6
records that `/manifest.json` 404s on pizzint and on all five AI competitors, so
none of them has a home-screen icon at all.

It is generated from `state.json` on every build rather than stored, so it
cannot disagree with the number on the page — the same argument
`site/build.mjs` already makes for the numeral favicon this replaces.

`litSlots(level) = 6 − level`. Read it once and the mark follows: DOOMCON counts
*down* toward louder, so "more lit" has to mean "lower number". A naive
`slot <= level` lights four slots at the calmest reading and one at the loudest,
which is the instrument running backwards.

### 1.2 Never colour alone, where there is no room for a word

This is the one place on the site where the usual answer — *the word is printed
beside it* — is not available. A 16px favicon has no room for a word. So the
reading has **three carriers inside the image and a fourth outside it**:

1. **Count.** How many slots are lit. Countable with the hue removed.
2. **Weight.** A lit slot draws at full stroke (4.8 units); an unlit one at 55%
   of that and at 30% alpha. Survives a greyscale repost.
3. **Hue.** The heat ramp. Confirmation, never the reading.
4. **The tab title**, which `layout.mjs` already emits as
   `DOOMCON 4 — ROUTINE · AI activity tempo index`. The mark is never
   unlabelled in situ.

### 1.3 The grid, and why the numbers are what they are

64 units. Centre (32, 32). Every asset is this one drawing at a different size
on a different ground, which is the whole reason the set reads as one mark
rather than as a family of related ones.

```
ring        r 28,  stroke 3.4
slots       r 23,  stroke 4.8,  drawn arc 5.6,  from 16°, step 37° (y down)
LED         r 7.0, with a 2.6-unit collar in the ground colour
```

At 16px a 64-unit grid is **4 units per device pixel**, and the whole design is
a fight for one clear pixel between the slots at that size:

- 37° at r=23 is **14.85 units** of arc between slot centres.
- Each slot occupies 5.6 (arc) + 4.8 (round caps) = **10.4 units**.
- That leaves a **4.45-unit gap = 1.11 device pixels** of clear space at 16px.

Below about 4 units of gap the slots blur into one smear at 16px and carrier 1
stops being countable. Every other number is downstream of that one: the radius
is as large as the housing allows (23 + 2.4 = 25.4 against the ring's inner edge
at 26.3), and the slot length is whatever is left once the gap is paid for.

### 1.4 What ships

| asset | size | note |
|---|---|---|
| `favicon.svg` | 64 grid | rounded ground, level-lit |
| `favicon-32.png` | 32×32 | for the engines that still prefer a raster icon |
| `apple-touch-icon.png` | 180×180 | **PNG is not optional** — iOS ignores SVG entirely |
| `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` | manifest icons, `purpose: any` |
| `icon-maskable-512.png` | 512×512 | 20% padding, because Android may crop to the inscribed circle |
| `manifest.webmanifest` | — | `start_url` and `scope` carry `BASE_PATH` |
| `og-default.png` | 1200×630 | the share card for every page with no card of its own |
| `og-default.svg` | 1200×630 | the vector original, for a press kit |

Total **96 KB** for the nine, regenerated every build, byte-identical for a
given level (verified across two separate Node processes).

### 1.5 The rasteriser, and why a zero-dependency repo grew one

`CONTRACT.md` §1 forbids npm dependencies and `collector/card.mjs` settled the
matter for share cards by emitting SVG and letting a browser rasterise. That
answer does not reach here, because two assets **must** be raster or they do not
work at all: the apple-touch-icon (there is no SVG path to an iOS home screen),
and the OG image — `site/build.mjs:314` already warns, in this repo's own words,
that *"X and most crawlers will not render an SVG og:image"*, and
`COMPETITIVE.md` §1.4 records the resulting blank share card as **our largest
single acquisition hole**.

So `site/brandmarks.mjs` carries ~180 lines of rasteriser. `node:zlib` and
nothing else. Analytic coverage rather than supersampling: every primitive it
needs — round-capped polylines, discs, rounded rectangles — has a cheap exact
signed distance, so a pixel's coverage is `clamp(0.5 − d, 0, 1)` and the edges
are clean at 16px for no sampling budget at all.

It also carries a **stroke alphabet**: 40 glyphs of straight segments on a 6×10
grid, which is every character the OG image sets and nothing else. It is a
stencil deliberately — `VOICE.md` §2 asks for *"an instrument room, not an
oracle"*, and a stencil is what instrument rooms letter things with. **It is not
a typeface.** It sets six short lines on one image. Body copy is Inter Tight and
JetBrains Mono, set by the browser, everywhere else.

### 1.6 The OG image is a reading, not a logo

Seven lines, every one a measurement or a name, no adjectives:

```
DOOMCON
AI EARLY WARNING SYSTEM
DOOMCON 4 / ROUTINE
40.7 OF 100 - 35-54 BAND
OBSERVED 00:04 UTC 24 SEP 2026
DETECTED EARLY, NEVER PREDICTED. WE JUST COUNT.
DOOMCON.WATCH
```

The stamp is not decoration. `VOICE.md` §2 habit 3 — *exact clock, always UTC,
never relative* — applies hardest here, because an OG image is cached by every
platform that touches it and is the surface most likely to be looked at long
after it was made. A figure with no clock on it claims to be current forever. It
is **omitted rather than faked** when no timestamp is passed; there is no code
path that prints a plausible one.

Every line is measured against its column and stepped down in whole points until
it fits, then **throws** if it still does not — the same discipline as
`collector/card.mjs`'s `fitSize()`/`auditCard()` pair, and for the same reason:
a line running off the edge of a share card is a defect nobody sees until it is
on somebody else's timeline.

> **Open item, and it is not this module's to fix.** The card burns
> `doomcon.watch`, which `COMPETITIVE.md` §1.4 measured as **not resolving**
> (`curl` exit 000). The domain is a parameter defaulting to `brand.DOMAIN`, so
> it is a one-line change the day the domain exists — but until then this image
> points at nothing, exactly as the existing SVG card does.

## 2. The icon system

`site/templates/_icons.mjs`. **49 marks in 8 sets, one grid, one stroke weight.**

```
viewBox   0 0 20 20      one size, every icon
live area 2.5 .. 17.5    nothing touches the box edge
stroke    1.7            one weight, everywhere
caps      round          one join, everywhere
fill      none           except ONE solid element per icon, and only where
                         "filled" is itself the information
colour    currentColor   one colourless sprite serves both schemes, the five
                         pillar hues and the heat ramp
```

### 2.1 What it replaces

The site drew its marks in **six** conventions across three grids, four stroke
weights and two rendering technologies. One concept — *pillar* — carried two
unrelated pictures on the same page, and a bare unicode `◆` is not an icon: it
is whatever the reader's font decides, it cannot be given a stroke weight, and
it arrives at a different optical size on Android than on iOS.

| set | n | what it covers | the convention it retires |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pillar` | 5 | the five `CONTRACT.md` pillars | `_reel.mjs` SIGIL (13 grid) **and** `_charts.mjs` PILLAR_GLYPH (unicode) |
| `state` | 4 | live · stale · dark · awaiting baseline | `.chip` borders alone |
| `datacentre` | 3 | operating · under construction · announced | `mapPage.mjs` locals |
| `direction` | 3 | up · down · unchanged | `_switcher.mjs` `▲ ▼ ◆` |
| `section` | 13 | every feature-bar and switcher destination | `layout.mjs` FEATURE_ART (16 grid) and `_switcher.mjs` unicode |
| `level` | 5 | DOOMCON 5 … 1 | five anonymous pips plus the heat ramp |
| `lab` | 9 | the eight frontier labs, plus a generic | `_labs.mjs` GLYPH (16 grid) |
| `kind` | 7 | the seven `NEWS.md` item kinds | nothing; this set is new |

### 2.2 Silhouette first, always

Every set differs in **shape** before it differs in anything else. Desaturate
the whole sheet and not one distinction is lost:

- **three—four source states** — filled disc / half disc / dashed empty ring /
  dotted ring with a centre dot.
- **the two dash patterns are not interchangeable.** `styles.mjs` already spends
  dashed on one meaning and dotted on another; `VOICE.md` §4 makes merging them
  the one edit nobody may make. `DASHED 4.5/3.2` = *this is not there* (a dark
  source, an unbuilt wall). `DOTTED 0.1/3.05` = *this is there and we cannot
  score it yet*. Measured against the ring's circumference (2π·6.5 = 40.8), the
  first lays five clean dashes with no stub at the seam and the second lays
  thirteen even dots. Both survive to 11px.
- **datacentre status** — one footprint at three degrees of completion. The axis
  is completion and nothing else, drawn as how much of one wall is solid.
- **the five levels** — one dial at five needle angles, 144° (calm, up-left)
  through vertical to 36° (loudest, up-right). A rotation is not a hue, so it
  survives greyscale and all three dichromacies.

`docs/VISITORS.md` §4 item 5 asks for the level rail to carry *"a glyph per stop
so it is never colour alone"*. The `level` set is that glyph. Today those five
stops are five anonymous pips plus the heat ramp, which is colour alone in the
one place on the site where the level is the whole point.

### 2.3 The one place the needle angles were wrong

The first draft swept the level dial 162° → 18°. At 162° the needle lies almost
along the dial's own chord at y=14.6 and merges with it, so DORMANT and
UNPRECEDENTED both came out of the harness as *"a dome with a bump"* and were
indistinguishable from each other — the worst possible pair to confuse. Pulling
each end in by 18° lifts both tips clear of the chord. Needle length 6.6 against
the dome's radius 7, so the tip never breaks the arc at ELEVATED.

This is recorded because it is the argument for the harness existing at all: it
was invisible in the source and obvious in the render.

### 2.4 Cost

One `<symbol>` sheet per page, each use site a 98-byte `<use>`. Inlining the
geometry instead costs 319 bytes per occurrence — 3.3×, and `_reel.mjs` measured
52 KB of that on the 200-row archive with five shapes. The full sheet is 13,123
bytes; `iconSprite({ only })` lets a page that knows its own set pay for nothing
else. A same-document fragment needs no script and no second request, so it is
still correct in a prerender, in a crawler, and in a screenshot taken before
hydration — which is the constraint every visual decision on this site is
downstream of.

### 2.5 Decorative by default

Every mark on this site sits beside the word it illustrates: the pillar tag
prints `CMP`, the freshness chip prints `dark`, the switcher tab prints
`Substrate`. An icon that announces "Compute & Capital" next to the text
"Compute & Capital" is a screen reader reading everything twice. `label` is
passed only where a mark genuinely stands alone.

## 3. The leader marks — and the line they do not cross

`site/templates/_avatars.mjs`. Eight emblems, one per named principal, plus a
ninth frame that belongs to nobody.

### 3.1 The line, stated plainly

**These are abstract and emblematic. They are not likenesses and they are not
caricatures of real people's faces.**

The brief that produced this file pointed at pizzint.watch — which ships a
cropped photograph of Pete Hegseth as a button and a picture of Donald Trump in
its timeline — and asked for "little trumps". That device works for them because
their whole page is a joke about pizza deliveries. Ours is not a joke about
anything: `/race` prints a computed rank, a market probability and a "posture"
word beside each of these people's names.

A drawn face next to a computed label about the person reads as a claim about
the person. A caricature reads as a verdict on them. Neither is ours to publish,
because the whole moat is *vivid framing, honest number*, and a cartoon of a
real man's face next to the word SURGE is vividness that has crossed into the
number's lane.

So the brief's **spirit** is taken — more visual personality, more colour, more
fun — and its **letter** is declined. Three rules hold the line:

1. **The label is the identification. The mark is decoration.** Every avatar
   this module emits carries the person's name in text — visible by default, and
   visually-hidden only when the caller has already printed the name immediately
   beside it. **There is no code path that emits a bare mark with no name
   attached.** You are never asked to recognise a shape.
2. **The shape is the person. The colour is the lab, at a value that is the
   person.** See §4.4.
3. **Nothing is fetched, generated or random.** No portrait, no gravatar, no
   hashed identicon whose output nobody has looked at. Nine hand-drawn emblems
   and a two-letter monogram, all in the static HTML, byte-identical across
   builds (`CONTRACT.md` §4).

The practical wins come free: no network request, no licensing question, no
image to go stale when somebody changes job, legible at 20px, correct in both
schemes, and a rendering that survives a greyscale repost.

### 3.2 The nine emblems

| shape | person | lab | the character |
|---|---|---|---|
| `orbit` | Sam Altman | OpenAI | a ring with a bead on it — a node on an orbit |
| `aegis` | Dario Amodei | Anthropic | a shield, top edge notched into a crest |
| `prism` | Demis Hassabis | Google DeepMind | a cut gem: a diamond with the bottom point taken off |
| `rotor` | Elon Musk | xAI | an octagon inside a reticle, four ticks on the axes |
| `stadium` | Mark Zuckerberg | Meta | a capsule sealed at both ends |
| `bracket` | Yann LeCun | Meta | two brackets, closed along the bottom |
| `cell` | Liang Wenfeng | DeepSeek | a flat-top hexagon, beaded at the two side vertices |
| `squircle` | Arthur Mensch | Mistral | a squircle with the top-right corner cut away |
| `frame` | **nobody** | — | a plain rounded square, dotted and unlettered |

Two of those are the harness's corrections rather than the first draft:

- **`prism` was a plain diamond with two facet strokes near the top vertex.**
  The strokes were invisible at 20 and 26px and fought the monogram at 34, so
  the character moved into the outline, where small sizes can still see it.
- **`cell` was a point-top hexagon** — and `prism` is a point-top pentagon. At
  16 and 20px the two were the same picture with one side's difference in it.
  A hexagon turned 30° has a different axis from every other mark in the set,
  which is a difference that survives being 16 pixels wide.

`frame` is a ninth shape rather than a borrowed one on purpose. `avatarUnknown()`
used to draw the squircle, and now that the squircle is Mensch's cut corner,
*"no principal published"* would have been rendered in another man's emblem.

### 3.3 The honest empty frame

`data/race.json` publishes `"principal": null` for Alibaba Qwen. The three source
states on this site are never merged and the same discipline applies to a
person: *we have not published a name* is a different statement from *there is
nobody*, and neither is *here is a placeholder head*. `avatarUnknown()` renders
a dotted, unlettered frame and says which one it means.

A published null also beats our own table. The lab-id fallback exists for a row
that never carried a principal field; it must not fire when the collector has
explicitly published `null`, because the module maps a lab id to a person and
would reinstate a name the collector has stopped publishing. That is imputation
— the same move as treating a dark source as a zero — and it fails silently:
measured against the live file, nulling every principal changed the rendered
watch floor by **zero bytes**, because every name was coming from a table typed
by hand.

## 4. The palette

### 4.1 The ground

`site/styles.mjs` ships `--bg: #0b0c0e`, a near-black **grey**. The ground
measured off pizzint on 2026-09-24 is `#060c16`, a near-black **navy**, and on
an icon the size of a favicon the difference is the whole character: grey reads
as a disabled control, navy reads as an instrument that is switched on.

`brandmarks.GROUND` is `#060c16`. The site ground has not moved — `styles.mjs`
is not this pass's to edit — so today the navy lives only on the icons, the
manifest `theme_color` and the OG image, all of which have their own ground by
definition (they sit on browser chrome or on somebody else's timeline, never on
our page). The two are 5.0 ΔE76 apart, which is a shade rather than a clash.
Moving `--bg` is item 1 of the integration note.

## 5. Colour is never the sole carrier — and here is where it structurally cannot be

Simulated with the Viénot–Brettel–Mollon 1999 dichromacy matrices in linear LMS.
`dE76` is CIE76 ΔE\*ab; below about 5 a pair is effectively one colour.

| set | scheme | normal | protanopia | deuteranopia | tritanopia |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pillar | dark | 32.3 | 9.3 | 21.2 | **7.8** |
| pillar | light | 24.0 | 10.7 | **1.6** | **4.7** |
| level | dark | 27.4 | 20.3 | 15.6 | **9.3** |
| level | light | 18.0 | 6.4 | **1.0** | **4.8** |
| state | dark | 61.8 | 18.8 | 20.2 | **9.3** |
| state | light | 38.6 | 18.7 | 13.1 | **4.8** |
| person | dark | 10.7 | 6.9 | **2.8** | **3.8** |
| person | light | 7.5 | 6.0 | 5.5 | **1.6** |

The closest pair in each cell, for the rows that matter:

- **pillar / light / deuteranopia — 1.6.** Attention `#9a5a00` and Markets
  `#b3441e` are one colour to a deuteranope on paper.
- **level / light / deuteranopia — 1.0.** ACCELERATED `#a8450d` and ELEVATED
  `#9a5a00` are one colour to a deuteranope on paper. Green and red are the
  textbook confusion pair and this ramp runs straight through it.
- **person / light / tritanopia — 1.6.** Hassabis `#1a56d6` and Zuckerberg
  `#1d4ed8` — two of the three blues.
- **person / dark / deuteranopia — 2.8.** Hassabis `#5b9cff` and Liang
  `#7a8cff`.

**This is reported rather than fixed, and the reason is not resignation.**

For the person set it is structural. Eight marks over six labs, three of which
(DeepMind, Meta, DeepSeek) sit in one blue-violet region of the wheel that
`styles.mjs` assigned before this pass and that pairs each principal with their
lab's box on the watch floor. Pulling them apart breaks a relationship a reader
can check in exchange for a separation no reader was relying on — because the
emblems are eight distinguishable silhouettes in greyscale at 16px, verified in
§6, and the name is printed beside every one of them.

For the pillar and level sets in the light scheme, the values live in
`site/styles.mjs`, which this pass does not own. Both are listed in the
integration note. Both are already mitigated — every pillar surface carries its
sigil or its name, and every level surface carries the level's name and band —
but "already mitigated" is not the same as "correct", and a ΔE of 1.0 between
two adjacent bands of the scale the whole site is named after is worth an
explicit line in the integrator's queue rather than a shrug.

**The rule that makes all of this survivable, stated once:** every element that
spends a hue on this site also carries a silhouette, a dash pattern, a weight, a
count, or the word itself. Desaturate the entire site and no fact is lost. That
is not a slogan; it is what §6 tests.

## 6. How this was tested

Everything below was run under
`docker run --rm -v "$PWD":/app -w /app node:20-alpine node …`, because Node is
not installed on the operator's machine (`CONTRACT.md` §1).

**The logo, at 16, 32 and 64px, at all five levels.** Rendered with the module's
own rasteriser — so what was looked at is what ships — then magnified with
nearest-neighbour onto one sheet. All five levels are countable at 16px: the lit
slots resolve as separate marks with about one clear device pixel between them,
and the LED is 2.75px across. The 162°→144° needle correction in §2.3 and the
four emblem corrections in §3.2 are the four things that sheet caught.

**Every one of the 49 icons, at 16, 32 and 64px.** The harness parses the
shipped sprite — including `stroke-dasharray`, so the dark ring's five dashes
and the awaiting ring's thirteen dots are compared as rendered rather than as
source. Every mark is legible at 16px and no two in a set collapse.

**The eight principal emblems, at 16, 20, 26 and 34px** — the three sizes
`_avatars.mjs` ships, plus 16px as a floor — in their per-person accent, with
the 15% wash `styles.mjs` paints behind them.

**The palette**, as §4 and §5.

**Determinism.** `brandmarkAssets()` run in two separate Node processes produces
byte-identical output for all nine assets, hashed. `CONTRACT.md` §4 requires it,
and a favicon that changed every build would also defeat every cache between us
and the reader.

**The live build.** `site/build.mjs` runs clean with all of this in place: 242
files, DOOMCON 4, score 40.7. The eight principals render on the watch floor
with `data-person` and their accents, and the one lab with no published
principal renders the `frame`.
