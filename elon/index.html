How we know it's real

Deepfakes are good enough that you can't judge a clip by looking at it. So this index doesn't look. It only lists videos uploaded by the channel that recorded them: Elon's companies, the AI labs, hosts who filmed their own interviews, and newsrooms with their own cameras.

Elon Musk: the title names him, or it's an event one of his companies held that he presents (shareholder meetings, AI Day, Starship updates). Superintelligence: the title says superintelligence, or, on an AI lab's own channel, the description does. AI leaders (Altman, Amodei, Hassabis, Huang and others): the title names them, or, on their own company's channel, the description does. All AI: every upload from an AI lab, and anything elsewhere whose title is unmistakably about AI. The index refreshes every hour and keeps everything it has seen.

Signs of a fake

  • A "live" stream promising to double crypto you send, or a QR code to claim a giveaway. Elon has never run one.
  • A channel renamed to "Tesla" or "SpaceX" with a different handle than the real one.
  • A clip only posted by a fan, "news" or compilation account, with no original source linked.
  • Lip movements that drift from the words, or a voice that's flat on emotional lines.
