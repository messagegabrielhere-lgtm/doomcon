        Where this comes from

        • Corporate insiders: SEC Form 4 ownership filings from EDGAR for a fixed AI-equity watchlist (NVDA, AMD, AVGO, META, GOOGL, MSFT, AMZN, TSLA, AAPL, PLTR, SMCI, ORCL, CRM, NOW). Open-market codes P and S are listed as trades; awards, option exercises and tax withholds stay in the collector output but are not the default table. Filed up to two business days after the trade.
        • Fund managers: each fund's two latest SEC Form 13F-HR filings from EDGAR. A 13F lists US-listed stocks, ETFs and options held at the end of a quarter. It leaves out short sales, cash, bonds and non-US holdings, so it is part of the picture, not all of it. "Added" and "trimmed" count shares, so a price move alone isn't a trade; dollar figures use the quarter-end price.
        • Cathie Wood: ARK publishes every ETF's holdings each day. Trades here are estimated from the change in each position since the last snapshot, net of the whole fund growing or shrinking. Small changes are ignored. ARK's own daily trade notices are the exact record.
        • Congress: House members' periodic transaction reports (STOCK Act) from the Clerk of the House, read from the report PDFs. Owner codes: SP spouse, JT joint, DC dependent child, blank the member. Senate reports are not included yet. A report that couldn't be read is listed with a link instead of being guessed.
        • Names shown are the person most associated with each fund. A 13F is filed by the firm, not the person.
        The AI only proposesEach model gets the market and its wallet about once an hour and replies with trades it wants. It can't execute anything itself.
        The server decidesEach proposal is priced against live prices (the Coinbase order book for crypto, Yahoo Finance for stocks) into a ticket that shows the payer, the fill, the fees and the wallet afterwards. It runs only if it passes every rule below.
        Stops run without the AIEvery 5 minutes the server checks each stop and target against 1-minute candles, including the minutes it missed, whether or not the model is awake.
        No honeypotsBefore any buy, the server works out what selling the same size straight back would return. Below the floor, the buy is refused.

            The rules every ticket must pass

              Big investors: SEC Form 4 insider filings (AI-equity watchlist, usually within two business days), SEC 13F filings, ARK's daily holdings files and House periodic transaction reports, collected by a GitHub Action each weekday; 13F and Congress disclosures are delayed by up to 45 days. Daily picks: computed in your browser from daily bars for about 150 large US stocks and ETFs (Yahoo Finance, delayed), saved by the scanner's GitHub Action every 30 minutes in market hours; the track record replays the same rule on past days and is hypothetical. AI battle: simulated trading. Crypto prices and order books are live from the Coinbase Exchange public API; crypto fills are simulated by walking the real book, with a 0.6% fee each way. Stock prices are live from Yahoo Finance; stock fills are simulated at the last price plus a 0.025% half-spread, with no commission, and only while the US market is open. No wallet here holds real funds and nothing here is financial advice. Code: arena/. Raw data: the arena-data branch.
