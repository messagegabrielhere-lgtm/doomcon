# What are the AI explainers?

> HTML: https://siren.watch/guides.html

Search often lands on a doom score, a countdown, or a DEFCON-shaped novelty page.
These pages answer the vocabulary questions without inventing a probability of harm.
SIREN stays an hourly activity count with a receipt.

## Guides

- [How does SIREN compare to DEFCON, the Doomsday Clock and p(doom)?](https://siren.watch/guide.html): Activity tempo vs readiness, symbolic clocks, opinions and title hijack — what each measures.
- [What is p(doom)?](https://siren.watch/p-doom.html): Named estimates on one chart. An opinion expressed as a percentage, not a receipt.
- [Is there an AI doomsday clock?](https://siren.watch/ai-doomsday-clock.html): The Bulletin’s clock vs AI-specific countdowns vs an hourly activity count.
- [How the SIREN AI activity index is computed · methodology](https://siren.watch/methodology.html): Every formula, pillar weight, and frozen-reference rule.
- [What is the history of AI risk?](https://siren.watch/history.html): Dated, sourced field notes — Good, Asilomar, the pause letter — not a forecast.
- [Which AI doom indexes can you compare?](https://siren.watch/compare.html): Side-by-side with DoomBench, Skynet Countdown, p(doom), PizzINT and Takeoff Tracker.
- [Which AI indexes does SIREN contrast?](https://siren.watch/vs.html): Directory of honest versus pages — activity tempo, not doom claims.
- [What are AI doom index alternatives?](https://siren.watch/alternatives.html): When this index is the right substitute — and when it is not.
- [Is AI taking jobs?](https://siren.watch/jobs.html): Register counts with sources and caveats — not a jobs forecast.
- [Is AI curing anything?](https://siren.watch/medicine.html): Trials, authorisations, and caveats — not a cure claim.
- [What free tools does SIREN publish?](https://siren.watch/free-tools.html): Verify receipts, alerts, export, OpenAPI — free instruments, not a doom calculator.
- [How do you check a SIREN reading yourself?](https://siren.watch/verify.html): Re-hash the last 12 public receipts in your browser.
