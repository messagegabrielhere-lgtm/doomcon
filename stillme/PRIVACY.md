# Still Me Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

Still Me lets you record your stories, opinions and the way you talk, so that people you choose can talk with an AI of you after you die. This policy explains what happens to what you record.

## The short version

**Still Me doesn't collect, upload, sell or share any of your data.** It has no servers and no accounts, and it includes no analytics or tracking.

## What stays on your iPhone

These are stored only on your iPhone, protected by iOS file encryption:
- What you record: interview answers, stories, writing, people, timeline, reply samples, and corrections.
- Your rehearsal conversations.

Voice recordings are turned into text on your iPhone using Apple's speech recognition. The audio isn't saved.

## The AI

Your AI runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence (Apple's on-device Foundation Models). What you record and what you say to it is never sent to Still Me or to any AI company.

## Sealed legacy files

When you choose to seal a legacy file for someone:
- **What's in it:** only the memories meant for everyone, or for that person.
- **How it's locked:** it's encrypted with AES-GCM, using a key derived from a 16-character code.
- **The code:** Still Me shows it to you once and doesn't keep it.
- **Who sees it:** you decide who receives the file, and how (AirDrop, Messages, email and so on). Anyone who has both the file and its code can open it, so keep codes somewhere safe, such as your will.

Without the code, the file reveals only who it's from and who it's for.

When someone opens a legacy file with its code, its contents and their conversations stay only on their iPhone.

## Your controls

- **Export:** save a copy of everything you've recorded at any time (You → Export my archive).
- **Delete:** erase everything from your iPhone at any time (You → Delete everything). Files you already shared are not affected; they still open with their codes.
- **Recipients:** someone who opened a legacy can remove it and their conversation from their iPhone at any time.

## Children

Still Me is not intended for children under 13.
