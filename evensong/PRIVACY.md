Evensong ("we") helps you record your memories so that people you choose can talk with an AI built from them after you die. This policy explains what we collect and what happens to it.

## What we collect

- **Your archive:** the things you choose to record. That means interview answers, stories, writing samples, transcripts of things you said aloud, corrections, your profile (name, hometown, birth year, how you talk, instructions, and off-limits topics), and your legacy settings.
- **Contact details you enter:** your email (optional, for reminders), your executor's name and email, and your recipients' names, emails, relationships, and personal notes.
- **Conversations:** messages sent to the AI, by you when rehearsing or by recipients after release. These are processed to generate replies. The server does not keep a transcript. Recipients' conversations are stored only on their own phone.
- **Audio** is transcribed on your device and is never uploaded or stored.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not track you across apps.

## How it's used

- **To store and back up your archive.**
- **To run the check-in process:** reminders to you, a confirmation request to your executor, and access codes to your recipients.
- **To generate AI replies.** Your archive and the current conversation are sent to **Anthropic, PBC** (the maker of Claude), which processes them to generate replies in your voice. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train Anthropic's models. [Link to Anthropic's current commercial terms and privacy policy.]
- **To send email** through [email provider], which receives the recipient's address and the message.

## Who can see what

- **Before release:** only you.
- **After release:** each recipient you chose can talk with the AI. The AI draws only on memories you marked for everyone or for that specific person.
- **Your executor** sees your name and the names and relationships of your recipients, but nothing from your archive.

## Your controls

- **Export** your archive at any time (You → Export my archive).
- **Delete** everything, on your phone and on our server, at any time (You → Delete everything).
- **Revoke** a release if you're alive. This voids every access code.
- **Recipients** can remove the legacy and their conversation from their phone at any time.
