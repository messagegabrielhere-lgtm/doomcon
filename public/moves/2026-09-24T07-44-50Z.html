DOOMCON

Nobody knows the odds. We keep count.

DOOMCON measures how much is happening, not how bad it is. Levels describe observable activity tempo against this index’s own record. They are not a probability of harm.

The desk

  • IndexThe composite, the five pillars, the live signal feed.
  • The RaceFrontier labs ranked on live prediction-market odds.
  • NewsroomEvery story, scored on how many independent sources carried it.
  • MethodologyEvery formula and constant. Recompute the number yourself.
  • HistorySixty years of the same argument, dated and attributed.
  • ArchiveEvery scored observation, each with a hash-chained receipt.

Data

  • JSON APILevel, score, pillars, per-source health.
  • History JSONEvery scored observation as one file.
  • HealthPer-source success, honestly reported.
  • EmbedOne iframe. No script, no key, no tracking.
  • RSSAn entry per index move.
