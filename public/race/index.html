How this is computed

The rank
Every player is placed by one number and one number only: the live YES price on their leg of Which company has best AI model end of 2026?, resolving 2026-12-31. That event is a negRisk market — 15 mutually-exclusive legs, exactly one of which resolves YES — so a leg price is a live, money-backed probability for that lab and nothing else. We do not blend the probability with shipping and mindshare into a composite score. A blend would need weights no one can check against an outcome, and the resulting rank would be ours rather than the market's. The other columns are context. They do not move the order.
Cross-check columns
today is the same question at the nearest resolution date (Which company has the best AI model end of September?, 2026-09-30) — who holds the crown right now rather than at year end. The two disagree sharply and they are supposed to. kalshi is a different question on a CFTC-regulated venue: “Which companies will have a top-ranked AI model this year?”, whose legs are independent binaries and do not sum to 1. It lists 12 contracts and carries no leg at all for some of this roster, which is printed as “no market”, never as 0%.
Shipping
Three channels, published side by side and never added together, because they are biased in opposite directions and a sum would hide both. gh counts releases across a fixed basket of that lab's own repositories — which measures release engineering as much as shipping, since some labs auto-cut an SDK tag on every API change and others ship weights and never cut a tag. A ≥ means the feed's ten-entry ceiling was hit and the real number is higher. hf counts model repositories the lab created on the Hugging Face Hub — a vendor-side publication event, and structurally zero for a lab that does not publish weights. or counts new entries in the OpenRouter catalogue. That timestamp is when OpenRouter listed the model, not when the vendor announced it, and it is never labelled as a release date anywhere on this page. All three windows are 30 days.
Mindshare
Share of DOOMCON's own news corpus naming that lab, computed from the entity list the signal feed already published — the same extractor, so the two surfaces can never disagree about a headline. The window is 200 items spanning 33.4 hours, not the nominal 7 days: the corpus is capped at 200 items and on a busy week the cap binds long before the window does. The trend compares the recent half (128 items) against the prior half (72 items); both must carry at least 20 items or it is reported as too thin rather than computed anyway. Shares do not sum to 100% — one item naming two labs counts for both — and only 40 of 200 items name any lab at all, because most of the corpus is research preprints. This is share of our corpus. It is not share of the internet.
Loudness
The brief asked for the public posting cadence of each principal, from feeds we can legitimately fetch. Applied honestly that leaves almost nothing, and the empty column is the finding. Six of these eight people post on X, and scraping X carries an explicit permanent-suspension penalty under its developer terms — so that door is closed, not hard. Facebook and Instagram expose no free per-person feed. One legitimate feed exists and it is published by the principal himself; where it is absent, the reason is printed on the row rather than replaced with a zero.
Manifold
The only comparable Manifold market carries 1,117 MANA of play money against the floor of 20,000. Published as a measured exclusion, not omitted. The probe is re-run every build rather than asserted once in a comment, so the exclusion cannot quietly go stale.

What a blank cell means

  • no marketThis venue lists no contract for this player. That is not a low probability — nobody is offering the bet.
  • no refThe venue has no price from a week ago to difference against. Not zero movement — no reference point.
  • not usedThis player does not publish to this channel at all. Different from having published nothing this month.
  • DARKThe fetch or the parse failed on this run. The value is unknown, and it is never filled in.
  • too thinOne half of the news window carried too few items for a share comparison to mean anything.
  • 0We looked, the channel answered, and the answer is genuinely zero. This is the only one of the six that is a measurement.

DOOMCON

Nobody knows the odds. We keep count.

DOOMCON measures how much is happening, not how bad it is. Levels describe observable activity tempo against this index’s own record. They are not a probability of harm.
